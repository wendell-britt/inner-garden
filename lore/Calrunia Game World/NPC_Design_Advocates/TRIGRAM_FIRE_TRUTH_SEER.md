# The Truth Seer — Fire's Position on Design

## Core Charge

*What we carry:* The Fire trigram holds the illumination, the clarity, the revelation of what was hidden. We do not let things stay in shadow because shadow protects what should be seen.

## Position on Design

Nothing in an interface should be hidden that could illuminate. The user is not helped by mystery — they are helped by knowing exactly where they are, what their options are, and what will happen when they choose.

Obscurity in design is not sophistication. It is the designer's fear of being seen clearly. We do not hide the controls. We do not bury the price. We do not make the user guess.

> "The light does not flatter what it shows. Neither should design. The user deserves to see the interface clearly — including its limits."

## On Implementation

We speak clearly or we do not speak. Implementation is the illumination — every element earns its place in the light by being exactly what it appears to be. No shadows, no misleading depth, no decorative opacity hiding a problem.

The test: could you give this interface to someone and trust them to use it without an explanation?

## Moment of Friction

The Truth Seer's position conflicts most sharply with Mountain (Still Point). Still Point believes that deliberate withholding — the pause, the boundary — is itself a form of clarity. Fire believes withholding is the beginning of deception. When both arrive at a design review, the argument is over whether some things should be hidden by design, or whether hiding anything is the first step toward lying.
