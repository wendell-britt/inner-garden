## The five nations (names are closed canon)

| Nation | EA channel (primary emotion family) | National sect (orthodox trigram) | Secondary trigram (via cultivation) | Border / floating context |
|--------|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------|--------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Argyra** | Metal / Fear | **Lake** | Mountain | Mountain border sect sits **Argyra ↔ Meridia** |
| **Pyrakanth** | Fire / Anger | **Fire** | Thunder | Thunder border sect sits **Pyrakanth ↔ Virelune** |
| **Virelune** | Wood / Joy | **Wind** | Thunder | Same Thunder border (rival tension with Pyrakanth in lore) |
| **Meridia** | Earth / Neutrality | **Earth** | Mountain | Mountain border (shared stillness axis with Argyra) |
| **Lamenth** | Water / Sadness | **Water** | Wind | Depth + grief that *moves through* |

**Fifth nation in group art:** **Virelune** — not missing from canon; it was missing from *this repo’s* text until this file.

## Eight sects (institutions)

| Sect | Trigram | Type | Territory / behavior |
|------|---------|------|----------------------|
| Argyra | Lake | National | Nation of Argyra |
| Pyrakanth | Fire | National | Nation of Pyrakanth |
| Virelune | Wind | National | Nation of Virelune |
| Meridia | Earth | National | Nation of Meridia |
| Lamenth | Water | National | Nation of Lamenth |
| *Floating* | Heaven | Floating | No fixed ground; seasonal gatherings |
| *Border* | Mountain | Border | Argyra ↔ Meridia; neutral in peace, chooses side in war |
| *Border* | Thunder | Border | Pyrakanth ↔ Virelune; initiative / fast-shifting |

**Design principle:** National orthodoxy ≠ only trigram a player may pursue; border + floating sects exist so any archetype has a legitimate in-world path.

## Pantheon (high level)

- **15 Aspected Gods** = 5 nations × 3 aspects (**Satisfaction**, **Dissatisfaction**, **Neutral** per nation).  
- Each nation’s **three aspects are its three First Cultivators** (same 15 — recognized across Calrunia, not “owned” by one nation’s theology alone).  
- Gods are **recognized** by nations, not invented by them (First Cultivators).  
- **Documented in this repo (examples):** Argyran triad — Verathane (Satisfaction), Kerath (Dissatisfaction), Caelath (Neutral); Pyrakanth Dissatisfaction — **Cindrel**.  
- **Not fully named in-repo:** the other twelve aspect-names remain to be written or imported from vault specs as you promote lore.
