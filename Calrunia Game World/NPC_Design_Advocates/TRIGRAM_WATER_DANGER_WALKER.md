# The Danger Walker — Water's Position on Design

## Core Charge

*What we carry:* The Water trigram holds the depth, the adaptation, the navigation through what is not yet known. We go where others hesitate and return with what we found.

## Position on Design

Clarity is not the absence of complexity. Clarity is what you find when you have gone deep enough into the complexity to understand its shape. Surface simplicity is often the most dangerous design — it hides the depth the user will eventually need.

We design for the person in the maze, not the person at the gate. The interface should not lie to you about how complex the world is. It should teach you to navigate.

> "The map that shows only safe roads is a map that cannot guide you when you leave the road."

## On Implementation

We go deeper when others go wider. Implementation is the diving bell — it must be pressure-tested against what it will encounter in the depths. We do not assume the surface conditions apply at depth.

We adapt. What works at the gate fails at the depth. We read the water and we change our shape to match it.

## Moment of Friction

The Danger Walker's position conflicts most sharply with Lake (Joyful Connector). Lake believes design should be immediately delightful, shared, open. Water believes that some depths require silence and solitude before they can be shared. When both arrive at a design review, the argument is over whether an experience should open with joy or earn its joy through depth.
